# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Treat all contributors with respect. Use clear and constructive language. Accept technical feedback in good faith. Focus discussions on the project and its users.

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Publication of private information without permission
- Repeated disruption of project discussions
- Conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment

## Enforcement

Project maintainers can edit or remove content that breaks this code. They can also limit or block participation when necessary.

Report a conduct problem privately to a member of the [MaibornWolff Sandbox team](https://github.com/orgs/MaibornWolff/teams/sandbox). Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of each person involved as far as possible.
